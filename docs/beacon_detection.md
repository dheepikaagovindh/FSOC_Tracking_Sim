**Team PHARO — Smart India Hackathon (SIH26169)**  
**Module 5 Technical Specification**

## 1. Purpose & Overview
The **Beacon Detection & Centroiding Module** implements high-precision optical signal processing to detect, segment, and localize the optical transmitter beacon spot on the degraded focal plane observation frames generated by the Disturbance Module (Module 4).

It computes continuous sub-pixel coordinates $(\hat{u}, \hat{v})$, optical signal-to-noise metrics, and estimated line-of-sight angular bearings relative to the camera boresight axis:

```
DISTURBED SENSOR OBSERVATION (Module 4)
              ↓
[1. Background Noise Floor Estimation (Median & MAD)]
              ↓
[2. Dynamic k-sigma Thresholding & Peak Search]
              ↓
[3. Localized ROI Extraction (2R+1 × 2R+1)]
              ↓
[4. Sub-Pixel Centroiding & Gaussian Refinement]
              ↓
[5. Optical Signal Quality & Confidence Evaluation]
              ↓
[6. Boresight Angular Offset Estimation (θ_az, θ_el)]
              ↓
BEACON DETECTION TELEMETRY (For Future Module 6 Kalman Filter)
```

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Strict Architectural Boundary**: Module 5 performs frame-by-frame optical detection and centroid localization. It **does NOT** implement multi-frame Kalman state filtering (Module 6), PID gimbal control (Module 8), FSM re-acquisition logic, dataset generation, or closed-loop gimbal actuation.

## 2. Input / Output Data Contract

### 2.1 Input Contract
- Disturbed 2D sensor image array $I \in \mathbb{R}^{H \times W}$ (`uint8`, $[0, 255]$) from Module 4.
- Camera optical intrinsics: focal length $(f_x, f_y)$ and principal point $(c_x, c_y)$ from Module 3.
- Detection configuration (`DetectionConfig`): algorithm mode, $k_\sigma$ multiplier, minimum PNR, minimum spot flux, ROI radius $R$, sub-pixel refinement toggle.
- Ground truth coordinates $(u_{\text{true}}, v_{\text{true}})$ used *strictly* for offline/HUD error benchmarking ($\Delta r = \sqrt{\Delta u^2 + \Delta v^2}$); never supplied to the detection algorithm.

### 2.2 Output Contract (`DetectionResult`)
- Detection status flag (`detected: bool`).
- Sub-pixel focal plane coordinates: $(\hat{u}, \hat{v})$ in pixels.
- Boresight angular bearings: $(\hat{\theta}_{\text{az}}, \hat{\theta}_{\text{el}})$ in degrees.
- Spot bounding box: `BoundingBox(x_min, y_min, x_max, y_max, width, height, center_x, center_y)`.
- Optical signal quality metrics: `SignalMetrics(peak_intensity, background_mean, background_std, threshold, pnr, snr_db, flux, confidence)`.
- Ground truth localization errors (if ground truth available): `DetectionError(error_u, error_v, radial_error, error_azimuth_deg, error_elevation_deg)`.
- Execution latency in milliseconds.

## 3. Mathematical Centroiding Models

### 3.1 Robust Background Noise Floor Estimation
Optical beacon spots occupy $< 1\%$ of the sensor image area. The background noise floor is robustly estimated using Median and Median Absolute Deviation (MAD), which are immune to bright outlier beacon pixels:

$$\mu_{\text{bg}} = \operatorname{median}(I)$$

$$\operatorname{MAD}(I) = \operatorname{median}\left( |I - \mu_{\text{bg}}| \right)$$

$$\sigma_{\text{bg}} = 1.4826 \cdot \operatorname{MAD}(I)$$

For clean images where $> 50\%$ of pixels are zero, sample statistics over the lower 95th percentile are used with an enforced minimum floor $\sigma_{\text{bg}} \ge 0.5$ for numerical stability.

---

### 3.2 Dynamic $k$-$\sigma$ Statistical Thresholding
The segmentation intensity threshold $T$ is adapted dynamically per frame:

$$T = \mu_{\text{bg}} + k_\sigma \cdot \sigma_{\text{bg}}$$

A candidate peak $(x_{\text{peak}}, y_{\text{peak}})$ is confirmed if:
$$I(x_{\text{peak}}, y_{\text{peak}}) \ge T \quad \text{and} \quad \text{PNR} = \frac{I(x_{\text{peak}}, y_{\text{peak}}) - \mu_{\text{bg}}}{\sigma_{\text{bg}}} \ge \text{min\_pnr}$$

---

### 3.3 Localized ROI Extraction
To prevent distant sensor noise spikes or dark-current hot pixels from biasing the centroid, a $(2R+1) \times (2R+1)$ sub-window is cropped around the candidate peak:

$$\text{ROI}(y, x) = I(y_{\text{peak}} - R \dots y_{\text{peak}} + R, \, x_{\text{peak}} - R \dots x_{\text{peak}} + R)$$

---

### 3.4 Intensity-Weighted Center of Mass (CoM)
Within the localized ROI, the background-subtracted Center of Mass calculates the sub-pixel spot centroid:

$$W(y, x) = \max\left(0, \, \text{ROI}(y, x) - T\right)$$

$$S = \sum_{(y, x)} W(y, x) \quad (\text{Integrated Spot Flux})$$

$$\hat{x}_{\text{rel}} = \frac{\sum_{(y, x)} x \cdot W(y, x)}{S}, \quad \hat{y}_{\text{rel}} = \frac{\sum_{(y, x)} y \cdot W(y, x)}{S}$$

$$\hat{u} = x_{\text{origin}} + \hat{x}_{\text{rel}}, \quad \hat{v} = y_{\text{origin}} + \hat{y}_{\text{rel}}$$

---

### 3.5 2D Gaussian Sub-Pixel Peak Refinement
For Gaussian Point Spread Functions (PSFs), sub-pixel refinement is computed using a 3-point log-parabolic fit around the local peak $(x_0, y_0)$:

$$\delta x = \frac{\ln I(x_0 + 1) - \ln I(x_0 - 1)}{2 \left( 2\ln I(x_0) - \ln I(x_0 + 1) - \ln I(x_0 - 1) \right)}$$

$$\delta y = \frac{\ln I(y_0 + 1) - \ln I(y_0 - 1)}{2 \left( 2\ln I(y_0) - \ln I(y_0 + 1) - \ln I(y_0 - 1) \right)}$$

$$\hat{u}_{\text{gauss}} = x_{\text{origin}} + x_0 + \operatorname{clamp}(\delta x, -0.5, 0.5)$$

$$\hat{v}_{\text{gauss}} = y_{\text{origin}} + y_0 + \operatorname{clamp}(\delta y, -0.5, 0.5)$$

---

### 3.6 Signal Quality & Confidence Metrics

- **Peak-to-Noise Ratio (PNR)**:
  $$\text{PNR} = \frac{I_{\max} - \mu_{\text{bg}}}{\sigma_{\text{bg}}}$$

- **Signal-to-Noise Ratio (SNR dB)**:
  $$\text{SNR}_{\text{dB}} = 10 \log_{10}\left( \frac{S}{\text{Area}_{\text{ROI}} \cdot \sigma_{\text{bg}}^2} \right)$$

- **Detection Confidence Score ($c \in [0.0, 1.0]$)**:
  $$c = 0.60 \cdot \sigma_{\text{sig}}(\text{PNR}) + 0.25 \cdot \text{Contrast} + 0.15 \cdot \sigma_{\text{sig}}(\text{Flux})$$

---

### 3.7 Boresight Angular Bearings
Using pinhole optical intrinsics $(f_x, f_y, c_x, c_y)$, the apparent azimuth and elevation bearings of the optical beacon relative to the camera boresight line of sight are:

$$\text{norm}_x = \frac{\hat{u} - c_x}{f_x}, \quad \text{norm}_y = \frac{c_y - \hat{v}}{f_y}$$

$$\hat{\theta}_{\text{az}} = \operatorname{atan2}(\text{norm}_x, 1.0) \times \frac{180^\circ}{\pi}$$

$$\hat{\theta}_{\text{el}} = \operatorname{atan2}\left(\text{norm}_y, \, \sqrt{\text{norm}_x^2 + 1.0}\right) \times \frac{180^\circ}{\pi}$$
